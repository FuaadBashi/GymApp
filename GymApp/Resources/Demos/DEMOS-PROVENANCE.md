# Demonstration pictures

Generated by `tools/build_demos.py` from `tools/demo_map.json`; `demos.json` lists every
asset's source, author, licence and changes, and the app shows that credit under each picture.

- **Everkinetic** (https://github.com/everkinetic/data, commit 446bb9a), CC BY-SA 4.0:
  line illustrations of each exercise's rest and effort positions. Downscaled to 480 px.
- **wger** (https://wger.de), per-image licence and author from its API (CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0):
  downscaled to 480 px and saved as JPEG; animated images reduced to their full-canvas frames.

Not used: the free-exercise-db photos (no source given, decision #14), and wger images in a
commercial 3D-render style without a named author.
